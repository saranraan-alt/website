Insurance Plans Curated by Our Insurance Associates

Every plan below is explained in plain language by the insurance associates at DHANVIN ASSOCIATES. We compare leading insurers, disclose the trade-offs, and help you buy only what you truly need.

Life Insurance

Protect Your Family's Income & Dreams

Life insurance is the foundation of every protection plan our insurance associates build. We begin by calculating your Human Life Value — income, liabilities, children's education, and your spouse's future needs — and then match you with the right product type.

Term Insurance

Pure protection at the lowest cost. A ₹1 crore cover can cost less than a family dinner each month when bought young. We compare claim-settlement ratios, premium waivers, and riders such as accidental death and critical illness benefits.

Endowment & Money-Back Plans

For savers who want guaranteed returns alongside cover, we shortlist plans with strong bonus track records and explain the real internal rate of return before you commit.

ULIPs & Child Plans

Market-linked options for long-term goals. Our insurance associates show you fund performance, charges, and lock-ins side by side so there are no surprises at maturity.

Health Insurance

Hospital Bills Should Never Break a Family

Medical inflation in India runs above 12% a year. Our insurance associates design health covers that absorb that shock, with cashless networks near your home and realistic room-rent limits.

Individual & Family Floater Plans

Floaters cover the whole family under one sum insured — ideal for young households. We explain co-payments, sub-limits, and restoration benefits in plain words.

Senior Citizen & Top-Up Covers

Specialised plans for parents above 60, plus super top-ups that raise your total cover dramatically for a modest premium. We handle pre-existing disease disclosures carefully so claims are never rejected on technicalities.

Motor & General Insurance

On the Road and at Home, Covered

From a scooter to a goods carrier, our insurance associates arrange motor policies with the right Insured Declared Value and add-ons like zero depreciation, roadside assistance, and engine protection.

Car & Two-Wheeler Insurance

Same-day policy issuance, renewal reminders before expiry, and hands-on help during accident claims — including surveyor coordination and garage networks.

Home, Travel & Shop Insurance

Protect your house against fire and burglary, your trips against cancellations and medical emergencies abroad, and your business premises against stock and liability losses. Small premiums, enormous peace of mind.

Our Method

How We Help You Choose the Right Plan

Every recommendation at DHANVIN ASSOCIATES follows a documented four-step process. First, a needs analysis: we map your income, dependants, loans, and existing covers. Second, a market scan: our insurance associates shortlist at least three policies from different insurers that fit the brief. Third, a comparison meeting where we explain premiums, exclusions, waiting periods, and claim-settlement history — in writing. Fourth, onboarding and annual reviews, because the right plan today may be too small five years from now.

This process is free of charge and free of pressure. If the best advice is "you already have enough cover," that is exactly what our insurance associates will tell you.
