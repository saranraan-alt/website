Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you charge for consultations?

No. Every first consultation with the insurance associates at DHANVIN ASSOCIATES is completely free. We earn commissions from insurers only if you choose to buy a policy through us, and we disclose this openly.

Can you help with a claim from a policy bought elsewhere?

Yes. Our claim-assistance desk regularly helps policyholders whose agents have moved on. Bring your policy document and claim papers, and our insurance associates will guide you through the process step by step.

How quickly can a policy be issued?

Motor policies are usually issued the same day. Health and term life policies depend on medical tests and insurer underwriting, typically three to ten working days. We track every application and keep you informed.

Do you serve clients outside the city?

Absolutely. Most of our process — comparison, documentation, and payment — can be completed digitally, and our insurance associates serve families across India through video calls and couriered paperwork.
