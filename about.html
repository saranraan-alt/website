About DHANVIN ASSOCIATES

The story, values, and people behind one of the most trusted teams of insurance associates in the region — serving families and businesses from www.dhanvinassociates.com.

Our Story

Fifteen Years of Keeping Promises

DHANVIN ASSOCIATES began with a simple observation: most people buy insurance they do not understand, from agents they barely know. Our founder set out to change that by building a practice where advice comes before commission. Today, our insurance associates serve more than 2,500 families, yet every client still receives the same patient, personal attention we offered on day one.

Over the years we have sat beside hospital beds during cashless approvals, helped widows receive claim cheques without running between offices, and reminded hundreds of clients about renewals they would otherwise have missed. These moments — not sales targets — define who our insurance associates are and why www.dhanvinassociates.com exists.

We remain independently owned, which means our loyalty belongs to you, not to any single insurance company.

What Drives Us

Mission, Vision & Values

M

Our Mission

To make quality insurance advice accessible to every household — from first-time earners buying a term plan to retirees managing health cover — through honest, jargon-free guidance from qualified insurance associates.

V

Our Vision

A future where no family in our community faces financial ruin because of an event that insurance could have covered. We measure success in claims settled and futures protected, not policies sold.

Our Values

Transparency in every recommendation, patience with every question, and presence at every claim. Our insurance associates disclose commissions when asked and document every comparison we make for you.

Credentials & Compliance

Licensed, Regulated, Accountable

DHANVIN ASSOCIATES operates under the framework of the Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India (IRDAI). Our insurance associates follow the regulator's code of conduct: needs-based selling, full disclosure of terms, and strict confidentiality of your medical and financial information. We maintain professional indemnity cover and a documented grievance process, so you always have a clear path to escalate any concern.

We are equally proud of our community work — free insurance-literacy workshops in schools, housing societies, and small businesses, because an informed policyholder is the best client our insurance associates can serve.
